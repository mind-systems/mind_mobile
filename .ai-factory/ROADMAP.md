- [x] **Fix two crossfade bugs: hung `play()` on first cycle and late crossfade start** — background: `_switchToPhase` is an async method called fire-and-forget via `unawaited`. - [x] **Adaptive crossfade duration based on incoming phase length** — currently `fadeDuration` passed to `_switchToPhase` is always `currentIntervalMs` (~1000ms for clock tick), regardless of how long the next phase is.
