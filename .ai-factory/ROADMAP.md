## Phase 44 — Breath dot motion: phase-velocity correctness + continuous flow

- [x] **Fix breath dot mistiming and stalls on uneven phase durations** — On asymmetric phases (e.g. inhale 2s / exhale 1s) the shorter phase covered only a quarter of its arc, snapped from ~75% to the target, and the dot hard-stopped at the bottom of each cycle. Four latent assumptions in `BreathAnimationCoordinator`/`BreathMotionEngine` (`packages/breath_module/lib/src/BreathSession/Animation/`) broke at once: (1) `_onStateChanged` re-armed phase duration only on `remainingTicks` change — skipped when adjacent phases share a boundary tick count → now also keys on `_previousPhaseIndex`; (2) fixed-ms damping → scale-invariant `_rampTimeConstantFraction` (ramp = share of phase); (3) `_isLinearLocked` froze an overshot velocity → removed, continuous self-correcting `remainingDist/remainingTime` controller guarded by `_minRemainingTimeMs`; (4) velocity zeroed at the 100% clamp + every `resetPosition` → 1.0 clamp removed (`normalizedPosition => _position % 1.0`), `resetPosition(preserveVelocity)` carries motion across `newCycle`/`exerciseChange`, drop-to-zero only at exercise `complete`. Residual phase-boundary hitch is render-side jank (`docs/breath/session/stutter-investigation.md`), not the engine. Spec: `.ai-factory/notes/129-breath-dot-motion-engine-fix.md`.
